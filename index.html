Case Study 01 Live Production

Smile Dental Studio — Phnom Penh

A modern, high-converting dental clinic web application engineered to address patient price hesitation and streamline appointment scheduling.

The Challenge

Local clinics frequently lose prospective patients who drop off due to hidden pricing or lengthy response times on Facebook Messenger. Receptionists were overwhelmed answering repetitive pricing inquiries manually.

The Technical Solution

Built an interactive single-page application with transparent treatment pricing and a secure serverless backend. When a patient selects a treatment and confirms, the system instantly formats a Markdown appointment brief and delivers it silently into the clinic staff's Telegram supergroup.
